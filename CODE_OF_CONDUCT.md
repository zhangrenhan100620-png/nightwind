# Code of conduct

Nightwind contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and differing levels of experience.
- Stop behavior that another participant reasonably identifies as harmful or disruptive.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. If an incident involves sensitive details, do not post them publicly. Contact the maintainer through an available private GitHub channel; if none is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact without naming people or describing the incident.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and the safety of affected participants. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
